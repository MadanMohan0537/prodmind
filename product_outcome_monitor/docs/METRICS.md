# Success metrics

- Percentage of shipped decisions with an active follow-through plan
- Median time from guardrail breach to human review
- Percentage of outcome reviews linked to source opportunity and experiment IDs
- False-alert rate on stable seeded fixtures
- Seeded reversal and breach detection rate
- Monitoring cadence adherence
- Percentage of decisions that receive a documented follow-up

Acceptance rate alone is not a success metric; teams should challenge alerts and document why.
