# Product metrics

| Metric | Definition | Initial target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verified lineage | Proposals linked to a verified Project 33 outcome | 100% |
| Reversible readiness | Approved proposals with valid rollback and triggers | 100% |
| Progressive rollout coverage | Approved proposals beginning below full scope | 100% |
| Enablement evidence | Proposals with training and communication evidence | 100% |
| Independent approval | Ready proposals with unique Product, Operations, and Governance reviewers | 100% |
| Controller latency | Deterministic response time at documented limits | p95 under 250 ms |

Targets describe intended operation, not results demonstrated by unit tests.
