# Product requirements

## Problem

Project 33 can verify that a planning-policy experiment met its declared prerequisites, but verified evidence is not a safe rollout plan. An organization still needs version, scope, progressive exposure, monitoring, rollback, enablement, and independent approval controls.

## Requirements

- Accept only a real Project 33 outcome review and preserve its identity.
- Require an active, versioned policy baseline and a higher semantic version.
- Limit the proposed scope to the baseline policy population.
- Require gradual rollout, bounded monitoring, and a monitor-linked rollback.
- Require training and communication evidence.
- Require independent Product, Operations, and Governance approval.
- Never activate, distribute, or roll back a policy automatically.

## Non-goals

This project is not a workflow executor, causal inference engine, HR performance system, or substitute for organizational change leadership.
