# Metrics

## Product metrics

- Verified recovery rate
- Blocked-close rate by failed control
- Mean recovery time versus approved maximum
- Observation-window and cadence compliance
- Exact factor-restoration pass rate
- Post-rollback incident rate

## Quality metrics

- Incorrect version or factor restoration detection
- Missing-class and duplicate-snapshot rejection
- Stale or gapped observation detection
- Evidence completeness
- False closure findings discovered during later review

These metrics evaluate the recovery record. They do not prove causality, statistical significance, future accuracy, or regulatory compliance.
