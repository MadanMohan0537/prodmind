# Metrics

## Scenario measures

- Objective-range violation
- Additions, removals, and retained commitments
- Total change count
- Capacity used and remaining
- Total Project 6 score after higher-priority constraints
- Number of subsets evaluated

## Product success measures

- Percentage of portfolio reviews that compare at least one scenario
- Percentage of accepted changes retaining evidence lineage
- Reduction in allocation-range violation after approved changes
- Override rate and documented reasons
- Percentage of locked commitments explicitly declared

## Guardrails

- Never report a scenario as applied unless the normal ranking workflow saves it.
- Never hide unmapped candidates by silently assigning objectives.
- Never equate score with realized value.
- Review dependency and lock assumptions before presenting to stakeholders.
