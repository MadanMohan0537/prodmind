# Product metrics

| Metric | Meaning | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Reference-class coverage | Share of completed items assigned to sufficiently sampled classes | Never hide insufficient classes |
| Duration absolute error | Mean absolute planned-versus-actual elapsed-duration error | Do not treat it as delivery probability |
| Capacity absolute error | Mean absolute planned-versus-actual capacity error | Do not use it to rank teams or people |
| Calibration adoption rate | Share of reviewed reports adopted | Adoption requires all checks to pass |
| Evidence freshness | Age of completed delivery observations | Stale cases block adoption |
| On-time rate | Completed items meeting declared deadlines | Descriptive, not causal |

Success means future estimators consult comparable actuals and document exceptions. It does not mean estimates become commitments or guaranteed forecasts.
