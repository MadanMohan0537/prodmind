# Metrics and formulas

For estimate `e` and positive actual `a`:

`absolute error rate = |e - a| / a`

For a base error `b` and calibrated error `c`:

`error reduction rate = b - c`

A positive reduction means the calibrated estimate was closer to the observed value. Zero means no change. A negative value means regression.

## Control checks

- **Exact lineage:** each Project 40 item exists exactly once in Projects 41, 45, and the reviewed outcomes.
- **Completed outcomes:** every delivery item is complete.
- **Accuracy thresholds:** calibrated duration and capacity errors do not exceed the declared maxima.
- **Calibration improvement:** both error reductions meet the declared minimum.
- **Exception outcomes:** each approved exception completed within both tolerances.
- **Evidence lineage:** customer, adoption, and reviewed outcome evidence remains present.

## Product success metrics

- Percentage of reviewed estimate bases retained
- Mean duration and capacity error reduction
- Percentage of exceptions within tolerance
- Time from delivery completion to effectiveness review
- Percentage of reports with complete evidence lineage

These measures describe the control process. They are not employee-performance metrics.
