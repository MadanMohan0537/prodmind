# Product requirements

## Problem

Project 45 proves that approved roadmap estimates used an adopted reference-class factor or a reviewed exception. It does not prove that those estimates were accurate after delivery. Without a closed-loop review, teams can continue using a compliant but ineffective estimate basis.

## Users and decision

Product operations, engineering planning, finance, and independent assurance use Project 46 after delivery. A named reviewer decides whether to retain the current estimate basis, adjust it in a later governed process, or collect more evidence.

## Requirements

1. Require matching approved Project 40, Project 41, and Project 45 records.
2. Require completed delivery evidence and one reviewed actual-workday outcome per item.
3. Compare base and calibrated duration/capacity estimates with observed outcomes.
4. Enforce declared accuracy and minimum-error-reduction thresholds.
5. Report exception outcomes separately without treating them as causal proof.
6. Preserve customer, adoption, and outcome evidence lineage.
7. Keep all estimate changes and factor changes outside this monitor.
8. Fail closed behind same-origin bearer authentication.

## Non-goals

- Predicting future delivery
- Changing a schedule or reference-class factor
- Establishing that a factor or exception caused an outcome
- Ranking teams or individuals
- Replacing finance, delivery, or statistical review
