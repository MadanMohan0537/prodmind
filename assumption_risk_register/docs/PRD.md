# PRD — Product Assumption Risk Register

## Problem

Important product decisions often rely on beliefs that are never written down, linked to evidence, assigned to an owner, or revisited. When outcomes disappoint, teams cannot tell which premise failed.

## MVP

1. Accept 1–200 assumptions across at most 50 recent runs.
2. Resolve assumptions only against selected canonical portfolio items.
3. Categorize assumptions and record importance and declared uncertainty.
4. Validate linked evidence and experiments against authoritative lineage.
5. Track owner, review deadline, validation method, status, and overdue state.
6. Produce a deterministic unresolved validation queue.
7. Preserve supported and refuted assumptions without changing upstream state.

## Non-goals

Probability estimation, automatic hypothesis generation, experiment execution, causal inference, automatic prioritization changes, and multi-tenant workflow.
