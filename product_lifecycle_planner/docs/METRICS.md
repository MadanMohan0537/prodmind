# Metrics

## Decision-quality metrics

- percentage of retirement plans with complete evidence lineage;
- affected-user and affected-segment coverage;
- dependency migration completion rate;
- notice-window compliance;
- communication coverage by affected audience;
- plans blocked before unsafe execution;
- customer incidents and exception requests after a sunset.

## Guardrails

- Never optimize for number of retired features.
- Do not infer low customer value from low aggregate adoption alone.
- Review suppressed Project 20 segments through an authorized privacy-safe process.
- Measure migration completion and customer harm, not merely whether a sunset date was met.
