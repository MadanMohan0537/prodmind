# Metrics

## Product metrics

- Re-entry trials with complete Project 38 lineage
- Pre-exposure declaration rate
- Stage-plan adherence rate
- Critical corrective-control coverage and operational rate
- Recurrence-monitor coverage by contributing condition
- Guardrail coverage and breach rate
- Sample-sufficient observation rate
- Human `continue`, `pause`, and `refreeze` rates
- Median time from approval to reviewed re-entry

## Quality metrics

- Invalid inputs rejected before evaluation
- Known failed checks that produce `blocked_continue`
- API requests rejected for missing authentication or wrong origin
- Test coverage across positive, boundary, and failure cases

## Interpretation

These are descriptive assurance measures. They are not probabilities, causal estimates, employee-performance measures, or proof that a recurrence cannot happen.
