# Metrics

## Product metrics

- Weighted evidence-gap coverage per research cycle
- Percentage of blocker findings scheduled
- Percentage of scheduled actions completed
- Percentage of covered findings later verified as resolved
- Time from integrity finding to research action
- Capacity utilization
- Override rate and documented override reasons

## Engine metrics

- `coveredPriority / totalPriority`
- Selected action count
- Used and remaining capacity
- Covered and uncovered target count
- Feasible combinations evaluated

## Guardrail metrics

- Research actions scheduled without original opportunity lineage
- Findings marked covered but still present after new evidence
- Capacity or effort unit changes within a planning cycle
- Repeated exclusion of the same customer segment

The team should not optimize only for coverage rate. Completion quality and verified gap resolution matter more than a full-looking plan.
