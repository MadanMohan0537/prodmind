# Metrics

## Migration metrics

- migrated and exempted coverage;
- remaining and blocked accounts or tenants;
- verified dependency coverage;
- delivered-notice coverage;
- open and expired exceptions;
- consecutive zero-use days;
- failed shutdown checks;
- post-target `overdue_hold` count.

## Guardrails

- Never optimize for fastest retirement at the expense of customer harm.
- Do not hide remaining users through cohort redefinition or unsupported exemptions.
- Do not count scheduled communication as delivered communication.
- Do not treat the Sunset HTTP header as guaranteed availability or unavailability.
- Review outcomes after retirement, including support load and migration-related incidents.
