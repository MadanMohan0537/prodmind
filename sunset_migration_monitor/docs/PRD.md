# Product requirements

## Problem

Lifecycle plans describe intended controls but cannot prove execution. Teams need a final reconciliation that catches unmigrated cohorts, live dependencies, undelivered communications, active exceptions, continued usage, and missing approvals before retirement.

## Requirements

1. Accept 1–50 snapshots linked to Project 21 consolidate or retire plans.
2. Reconcile 1–100 aggregate cohorts without customer identifiers.
3. Match every planned dependency to verified evidence.
4. Validate notice delivery, exceptions, sustained zero use, shutdown checks, and final approvals.
5. Preserve upstream lineage and expose every failed check.
6. Distinguish `hold` from `overdue_hold` without triggering an action.

## Non-goals

Identity resolution, notification delivery, migration execution, traffic control, feature shutdown, data deletion, legal interpretation, or automatic approval.
