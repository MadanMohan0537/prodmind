# PRD — Product Investment Assurance Review

## Problem

Product teams frequently close work at release while the original investment decision, realized benefits, follow-up actions, and accountable reviewer remain scattered or undocumented.

## MVP

1. Review 1–100 selected portfolio items from at most 50 runs.
2. Consume a valid Project 16 benefits report.
3. Verify evidence, decision and measurement completeness.
4. Record a named reviewer, date, rationale and explicit decision.
5. Require actions for `correct` and `escalate` decisions.
6. Track open and overdue actions.
7. Preserve all upstream identities and never mutate upstream state.

## Non-goals

Automatic go/kill decisions, causal inference, financial audit, employee evaluation, notifications, and multi-tenant authorization.
