# Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Complete review rate | Reviews passing every completeness check / reviews | Completeness is not business success |
| Benefit-measurement coverage | Reviews whose linked benefits are measured / reviews | Do not reward premature measurement |
| Corrective-action closure | Closed actions / actions due | Preserve cancelled actions separately |
| Overdue action rate | Open actions past due / open actions | Keep owner and date visible |
| Review cycle time | Days from benefit target date to review | Segment by investment type |
| Evidence lineage coverage | Reviews with retained evidence IDs / reviews | Missing lineage is an assurance failure |

Never evaluate individual performance from decision choice, benefit status, or action count.
