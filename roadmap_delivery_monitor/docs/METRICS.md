# Product metrics

## Delivery controls

- **Schedule performance index:** earned work divided by time-phased planned work.
- **Capacity overrun rate:** positive consumed-capacity variance divided by baseline work.
- **Deadline control rate:** items completed by deadline divided by completed items.
- **Dependency integrity rate:** actual starts with all dependencies previously completed.
- **Blocker aging:** open and overdue blockers by type and owner.
- **Snapshot freshness:** days since the most recent complete observation.

## Product effectiveness

- Continue decisions blocked by objective evidence
- Median time from a failed control to a named replan or escalation
- Percentage of schedule items with both source and delivery evidence
- Difference between final actual and planned capacity-days
- False-alarm review rate for configured thresholds

## Guardrails

- Never use the indices to score individual employees.
- Never call schedule performance a completion forecast or probability.
- Never describe the implementation as certified EVM.
- Never treat a human-entered work unit as independently verified truth.
- Never mutate schedules, assignments, estimates, or blockers.
