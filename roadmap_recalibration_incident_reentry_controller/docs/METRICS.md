# Product metrics

## Decision-quality metrics

- Re-entry reviews with complete Projects 48–50 lineage
- Post-incident publication within the declared window
- Corrective-action ownership and completion rate
- Critical-action completion before re-entry
- Preventive and detective action coverage
- Independent approval coverage

## Safety metrics

- Re-entry attempts blocked for stale, incomplete, or overdue evidence
- Attempts blocked for reuse of the failed version
- Attempts blocked for excessive initial exposure
- Attempts with complete rollback and monitor declarations
- Recurrence rate after approved re-entry

## Limitations

These metrics evaluate the governance record. They do not prove evidence truth, causality, statistical significance, future estimate accuracy, or compliance.
