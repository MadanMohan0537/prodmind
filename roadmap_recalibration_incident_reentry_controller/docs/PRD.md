# Product requirements

## Problem

Project 50 proves that rollback recovery succeeded, but recovery alone does not prevent recurrence. A failed recalibration can be retried without structured learning, completed corrective action, a cooling period, or independent approval.

## User outcome

Product, Delivery, and Assurance leaders can inspect one evidence-linked record and decide whether a new candidate may enter a bounded shadow-first evaluation.

## Functional requirements

1. Accept only a bounded Project 50 report with Projects 48–50 lineage.
2. Require a timely, chronological, evidenced and blameless incident record.
3. Require preventive and detective actions; block incomplete P0/P1, overdue, or re-entry-required work.
4. Enforce a cooling period, freeze, recovered baseline, new candidate version, and recovered scope.
5. Require shadow evaluation, bounded exposure, rollback readiness, and core accuracy/incident monitors.
6. Require independent Product, Delivery, and Assurance approval.
7. Preserve the named human re-entry, freeze, or escalation decision.

## Non-goals

The product does not execute rollout, lift a freeze, change estimates or factors, assign blame, establish causality, or certify compliance.
