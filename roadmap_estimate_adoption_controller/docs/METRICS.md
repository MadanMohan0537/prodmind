# Product metrics

| Metric | Meaning | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Controlled estimate coverage | Share of Project 40 items with exactly one application | Missing or duplicate records are rejected |
| Factor adoption rate | Share applying both Project 44 recommendations | Lower factors require a prior independent exception |
| Arithmetic reconciliation rate | Share matching declared base × factor within policy | Tolerances remain explicit and bounded |
| Exception rate | Share relying on an approved exception | Never interpret a lower rate as automatically better |
| Stale-calibration rate | Share reviewed against evidence older than policy | Stale calibration blocks approval |
| Review resolution time | Time from control record to named decision | Automation never supplies the decision |

Success means approved roadmap estimates have inspectable provenance and governed departures. It does not mean every estimate is accurate or guaranteed.
