# Metrics

## Product metrics

- Promotion-block rate: reviews where a failed gate prevents promotion
- Rollback-readiness pass rate: reviews with the exact target and approved recovery limit
- Evidence completeness: stages and class observations with retained evidence
- Decision latency: elapsed time from final stage to human review
- Incident guardrail breach rate

## Quality metrics

- Exact-version and exact-factor mismatch detection
- False promotion rate discovered during later audit
- Class coverage and paired sample sufficiency
- Candidate duration and capacity error versus concurrent baseline
- Test coverage of malformed, blocked, and human-action outcomes

Metrics describe the review process. They do not establish causality, statistical significance, or compliance.
