# Product requirements

## Problem

Project 25 can block a governance decision, but real organizations sometimes need a temporary, explicit risk acceptance while remediation proceeds. An undocumented bypass destroys the audit trail. Project 26 makes exceptions narrow, expiring, controlled, independently approved, and linked to remediation without changing the failed upstream result.

## MVP

- Consume authoritative Project 25 monitors.
- Target a real failed check or problematic obligation.
- Apply shorter maximum durations to higher-risk exceptions.
- Require evidenced compensating controls and an open remediation obligation.
- Require Product and Governance approval, plus Risk approval for high or critical exposure.
- Separate requester and approvers.
- Record approve, reject, and close decisions.
- Preserve governance-pack, portfolio-item, and customer-evidence lineage.

## Non-goals

The register does not waive law or policy, determine risk appetite, prove approval identity, change the upstream status, execute remediation, or create permanent exceptions.
