# Product requirements

Project 38 converts a verified Project 37 policy recovery into a blameless learning record and a controlled decision about whether a new policy trial may begin. It requires evidence-backed system conditions and lessons, owned measurable actions, completed P0/P1 work, no overdue actions, a cooling period, the recovered baseline, bounded scope, safety acknowledgements, and independent approval.

Outputs are `ready_for_reentry`, `blocked_reentry`, or `action_required`. The product never lifts a freeze, starts a trial, changes policy, assigns blame, or claims causality.
