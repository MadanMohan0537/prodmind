# Metrics

## Portfolio measures

- Alignment coverage by effort
- Allocation deviation from target shares
- Allocation concentration across objectives
- Underallocated objective count
- Overallocated objective count
- Unmapped selected opportunity count

## Product success measures

- Percentage of selected effort mapped to a reviewed objective
- Percentage of quarterly portfolio reviews completed
- Percentage of allocation exceptions with a documented decision
- Time from strategy change to portfolio reassessment
- Percentage of selected opportunities retaining evidence lineage

## Guardrails

- Do not optimize for 100% mapping by assigning meaningless objectives.
- Do not interpret effort share as realized business value.
- Track strategy and mapping overrides rather than hiding them.
- Reassess after material strategy, evidence, capacity, or outcome changes.
