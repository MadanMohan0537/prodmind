# Metrics

## Scenario measures

- Capacity overrun
- Unavailable selected-item share
- Broken dependency count
- Strategic allocation-range violation
- Scenario resilience score
- Weakest scenario

## Product success measures

- Percentage of portfolio reviews with at least one documented stress scenario
- Percentage of critical exposures receiving an explicit owner and review decision
- Scenario findings detected before commitment
- Accepted mitigation plans retaining evidence lineage
- Time from scenario change to portfolio reassessment

## Guardrails

- Do not treat scenario scores as probabilities.
- Do not reward teams for hiding difficult scenarios.
- Keep assumptions and adjustments visible.
- Never auto-remove work based on a stress result.
