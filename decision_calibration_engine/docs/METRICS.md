# Metrics

- Brier score: mean squared difference between stated confidence and binary resolved outcome.
- Mean confidence: average Project 6 confidence among resolved decisions.
- Observed success rate: share of resolved decisions with sustained status.
- Confidence bias: mean confidence minus observed success rate.
- Mean absolute error: average absolute forecast error.
- Resolution coverage: resolved decisions divided by all eligible decisions.
- Reliability-bin gap: average confidence minus observed rate within each confidence band.

Brier score combines multiple forecast qualities and should not be described as calibration alone. Small bins are unstable; Project 10 always reports their counts.
