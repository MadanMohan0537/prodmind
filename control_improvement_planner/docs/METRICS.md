# Metrics

## Product metrics

- Percentage of actionable recurrence risk covered by approved plans
- Percentage of selected actions with owners and verification windows
- Median time from recurrence detection to reviewed plan
- Capacity utilization and uncovered risk points
- Improvement actions later verified without recurrence

## Quality metrics

- Unknown-target rejection rate
- Dependency and capacity violation count (target: zero)
- Deterministic replay agreement (target: 100%)
- Evidence-lineage preservation (target: 100%)
- Approval attempts blocked below the declared threshold

## Guardrails

Do not reward teams for reducing finding counts by suppressing monitoring, lowering coverage thresholds, or combining unrelated findings. Risk points rank declared inputs; they do not measure probability, expected loss, compliance, or individual performance.
