# Metrics

## Product metrics

- Percentage of revert decisions with complete recovery reviews.
- Median hours from revert decision to initiation.
- Percentage of reversions completed inside their approved SLA.
- Percentage of close attempts blocked by missing or failing evidence.
- Median recovery observation days before closure.
- Percentage of closed reversions with uninterrupted monitor compliance.

## Quality metrics

- Deterministic test pass rate.
- Invalid lineage rejection rate.
- False-close rate discovered during later audits.
- Percentage of reviews with complete evidence references.

Metrics should be segmented by policy and scope. They must not be used to reward faster closure at the expense of recovery quality.
