# PRD — Product Benefits Realization Tracker

## Problem

Teams approve product work using expected outcomes, but often stop tracking at delivery. That breaks the trace from the customer problem to the value eventually observed.

## User and job

Product leaders, PMs and portfolio owners need to review whether a selected opportunity reached an agreed, measurable target, who owns follow-up, and which original evidence and decisions support the record.

## MVP requirements

1. Accept 1–100 benefit records and at most 50 ProdMind runs.
2. Resolve only canonical selected portfolio items.
3. Require unit, direction, baseline, target, dates, owner and attribution note.
4. Calculate progress deterministically for increase and decrease targets.
5. Preserve evidence, experiment, decision and outcome-review IDs.
6. Identify missing and overdue measurements.
7. Never aggregate unlike units or infer causal attribution.

## Non-goals

Financial forecasting, causal inference, automatic roadmap changes, employee scoring, cross-tenant collaboration, and automated source ingestion.
