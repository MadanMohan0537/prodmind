# Product metrics

- Percentage of completed sunsets with a reviewed observation window.
- Percentage with zero residual traffic.
- Customer-support and incident thresholds breached after sunset.
- Observed savings realization rate, kept separate by currency.
- Percentage with a tested rollback inside the declared recovery target.
- Open and overdue corrective actions.
- Human outcomes: close, extend monitoring, or restore service.

These metrics describe declared evidence. They do not prove causal impact or employee performance.
